Part I

Consent Form

CommonWell Healthcare Management System — Usability Evaluation
This study evaluates the usability of the CommonWell Healthcare Management System to assess how easily students and community members can navigate the platform and access health services. As a participant, you will explore the prototype, interact with features such as appointment booking, health records, medication tracking, vaccination tracker, and the admin panel, and complete the System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire. The activity takes approximately 10–15 minutes.

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may skip any item or withdraw at any time without consequence. All responses will be kept strictly confidential, anonymized, and used solely to improve the platform. Risk is minimal — only the time involved. Your feedback directly benefits the Barangay Halawig-Gogon community and gives you firsthand experience with a local health technology platform.

Course Instructor: Michael B. Amata · Computational Sciences Department

Part III

System Usability Scale (SUS)

10-item validated usability measurement instrument

The System Usability Scale (SUS) is a widely used tool in usability research that provides a quick and reliable measure of the perceived usability of a system. It consists of 10 statements that assess ease of use, learnability, and consistency.

1 — Strongly Disagree 2 — Disagree 3 — Neutral 4 — Agree 5 — Strongly Agree

1 I think that I would like to use this system frequently.

Part IV — Open-Ended Questions

1. What features or aspects of the CommonWell Healthcare Management System did you find most effective or helpful?

  • "Its ability to securely share patient data across different health systems."
  • "In critical situations, emergency doctors can quickly access important medical information, which is crucial for saving lives."
  • "Its ability to securely share patient information between healthcare providers — improving care coordination and allowing quick access to medical histories."
  • "The feature that tracks the progress of requests in the app (medical certificate and appointment/schedules)."
  • "It keeps you updated and provides a safe space to regularly check your healthcare progression."
  • "The profile and announcement aspects are helpful and effective."

2. What improvements can you suggest for the system?

  • "Simplify the interface for easier navigation to improve data accuracy."
  • "Enhance the user interface to be more user-friendly, improve system speed and reliability, and strengthen data security and privacy."
  • "Standardize the management of notes, images, and unstructured data — it varies greatly across modules."
  • "Keep updating and improving the website to provide better healthcare updates and plans for medical concerns."
