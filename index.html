Project Overview

What We Evaluated

◈

Heuristic Evaluation

Each group member independently evaluated the CommonWell prototype against Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics to identify design issues.

◉

Usability Testing

Conducted System Usability Scale (SUS) testing with actual respondents who explored appointment booking, health records, medication tracking, and more.

◆

Design Recommendations

Based on evaluation findings, we propose targeted improvements to enhance navigation, accessibility, and overall user experience of the platform.
